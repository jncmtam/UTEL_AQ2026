# Qualification targets and limits

Accepted circuits must preserve the canonical problem identity and supported
invariants. Numerical NumPy/Qiskit fidelity tolerance is 1e-9; allowed cardinality
leakage is 1e-9. These are simulator engineering tolerances, not hardware fidelity.

The 95% first-pass-validity aspiration and 20% relative quality/resource target
are provisional project goals. They are not predictions obtained from published
portfolio studies and are not sufficient evidence from three diagnostic cases.

A claim requires a prespecified financial tolerance, matched complete budgets,
strong classical/adaptive controls, independent cases and uncertainty estimates.
Failed proposals remain in the denominator. Unknown references are not optima.
Use original objectives after allocation; never normalize by arbitrary penalty
energy or equate syntax correctness with investment value.
