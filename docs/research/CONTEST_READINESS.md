# Contest readiness assessment

Reviewed: 2026-11-05. Assessment, not an official judging score or prediction.

Sources: [official site](https://ai-quantum.hvtc.edu.vn/),
[summary rules](https://ai-quantum.hvtc.edu.vn/the-le),
[Academy of Finance regulations distributed by Banking Academy](https://hvnh.edu.vn/medias/ctsv/vi/06.2026/system/archivedate/587a3f9d_CT%20AI%20Quantum%202026.pdf).

The published final rubric assigns20% practical problem resolution,15% technical/
data quality,20% product/demo,20% deployment feasibility,10% social impact/ethics,
15% presentation/defense. Simulation is allowed. Complexity alone does not earn
high marks. The regulations require Vietnamese presentation/materials and declared
AI assistance with substantial human intervention and ability to explain the work.
The official website lists the final for10November2026; organizer updates take precedence.

## Assessment of AGQO

Strong prototype foundation:220 tests, real Modal GPU receipts, audited compiler,
exported circuits, failure preservation and reproducible measurements.

The main weakness is demonstrated practical necessity. Both prompt arms produce
the same deterministic star. Guided/context financial scores tie. Pooled training
F improves1.29% against fixed QAOA but loses3.57% against allocation-aware swap.
Some model explanations are false. These findings do not establish incremental
LLM value, independent financial benefit or quantum advantage. Historical investment
data admission also remains blocked. A higher parameter count cannot certify these.

## Highest-value preparation

1. Demonstrate a complete portfolio-repair decision: mandate change, noncompliance,
   proposed trades, verified constraints and tracking/cost consequences.
2. Support the financial need with practitioner feedback and a concrete use case;
   a real pilot partner is not currently established by the repository evidence.
3. Validate a narrow measurable benefit against the appropriate strong comparator.
   Circuit adaptation efficiency is a candidate, not an observed contribution.
4. Resolve data suitability for the scope claimed. Label the conditional corpus
   as algorithm evidence; do not present it as investable historical proof.
5. Prepare Vietnamese submission/pitch materials separately from maintained English
   source/docs. Show the team's own decisions, modifications and understanding,
   disclose AI assistance and clarify permitted scope with organizers if uncertain.

Verdict: credible technical prototype, but the central practical LLM/QAOA value
claim is not ready for a convincing top-prize defense. A polished demo cannot
substitute for that evidence; negative results should remain visible.
