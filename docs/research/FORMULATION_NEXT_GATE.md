# Next technical gate: preserve the financial problem before circuit scaling

Date2026-11-06. Evidence-based specification, not an executed new experiment or
permission to dispatch another paid/model study. Read decision_audit_v1/FINDINGS.md.

## Why the present continuation stops model scaling

The complete numerical N20 references show11.07%/8.35% allocated regret from exact
proxy minimization, while local search reaches both numerical incumbents. The
conditional decision audit retains a5.81% detailed-guidance deficit, and a better
mean can accompany a worse expected minimum. The current source remains conditional
US research data; historical investability is not admitted. These are distinct
formulation, benchmark and data issues, not proof that every LLM or quantum method
must fail. No model upgrade is selected from these observations.

## Preserve one common financial reference

Keep continuous weights w and binary support x, tracking/cost objective F(w),
sum(w)=1, sum(x)=K, minimum_weight*x<=w<=maximum_weight*x, mandatory holdings/exits,
sector-weight limits and one-way turnover including cash. The existing convex
allocator and joint reference remain the common numerical finance truth.
Do not change to equal weights and call that a repair of the same task.

Before proposing a quantum encoding, write its projection from encoded variables
to (x,w), its feasibility relation and its objective error. On small exhaustive
instances compare financial optima, not only matrix energies or rank correlation.
Status unknown, allocation failure and selection feasibility stay distinct.

## Encoding alternatives to analyze before implementing a new solver

1. **Discrete weight/lot encoding.** Encode bounded portfolio amounts as well as
   selection. Quantify discretization regret versus continuous F, feasibility near
   sector/turnover boundaries, qubit/slack growth and gate cost. A lot-based variant
   is an explicit approximation/transfer experiment, not silently the primary task.
   A fixed-cardinality XY mixer alone does not preserve total money, weighted
   sector budgets or lot semantics. Do not promise a large simulation from a small
   encoding example.
2. **Binary/continuous decomposition.** Keep convex allocation explicit and derive
   a support subproblem from an objective-preserving decomposition or validated
   bounds. The existing [ADMM-related source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02069v2)
   is a precedent, not a convergence or quantum-speedup guarantee. Check whether
   the resulting binary step is just sorting/selection with easy group counts;
   reject a quantum-value claim if it is. Count all outer solves and repairs.
3. **Explicitly approximate F surrogate.** Fit/construct a bounded support surrogate
   only with declared allocation-query cost and an independently checked optimum-
   regret/error margin. Low prediction MSE alone does not protect the best portfolio.
   No exhaustive diagnostic F table may be advertised as free scalable preprocessing.

Choose an economic approximation margin from the intended tracking/transaction-cost
decision on development data, before evaluating a new method. A literature result
on a different objective does not justify that margin.

## Verify difficulty and decision alignment separately

- A large binomial support count is not evidence of practical classical difficulty.
  Compare exact/bounded MIQP and informed local search under matched complete budgets.
  Keep easy cases as correctness/ceiling controls. The audited base/exit optima require only one/two new positions from the original support; account for this repair radius instead of treating the full binomial count as operational hardness. Add new preregistered development
  families and financially motivated binding constraints before any outcome review;
  never manufacture a convenient hard case after seeing a quantum result.
- Define whether deployment returns the best candidate, an average portfolio, or
  a probability of meeting a financial threshold. Match the fitted functional and
  candidate budget to that decision; report the retained incumbent and all samples.
  The new conditional64-shot metric does not evaluate the unconditional adaptive
  576-shot policy. New fitting needs its own frozen study and noise analysis.
- Only after formulation and nontrivial sampler/solver headroom are established,
  compare LLM edits against equal-budget numerical edits, learned selectors and
  reuse. The model must add value beyond the scored examples it receives. Training
  an edit policy is a distinct proposed study, not an observed result here.
