# Financial research protocol

The primary decision is repairing an existing sparse US-equity tracker after a
mandate changes. Risk/return is a separate transfer objective. The LLM designs the
search circuit, QAOA samples supports, and a common allocator assigns weights.

Audit the dataset first, measure selection-versus-allocation distortion, establish
classical references, and then assess circuit design. Keep common-state decision
comparisons separate from continuous per-method account trajectories.

Read [dataset suitability](research/DATA_SUITABILITY.md),
[implementation protocol](research/IMPLEMENTATION_PROTOCOL.md), and
[results specification](RESULTS_SPEC.md). Historical market claims remain blocked.
